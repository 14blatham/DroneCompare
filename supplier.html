Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for leads?

expand_more
It's a transparent pay-as-you-go model. You only pay a small advertising fee each time a lead is generated on your behalf — the fee depends on the specific service and area. Fees are billed automatically via direct debit. There are no joining, monthly, annual, or exit fees.

Is there a commission on jobs?

expand_more
No, never. You earned the job, so you keep the reward. Once you receive the lead, you deal entirely directly with the client to negotiate, finalise the price, and collect payment. Operators pay only a small upfront fee to be listed and per-lead advertising fees.

Can I pause my account?

expand_more
Yes. You have complete control from your control panel. If you're fully booked, toggle your profile to 'Inactive' with one click. No leads will be sent, and no fees will be charged.
